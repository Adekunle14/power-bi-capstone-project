# 📊 SparkWave Electronics — Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

![SparkWave Electronics Dashboard](image%202%20%281%29.png)

## 📌 Project Overview

The **SparkWave Electronics Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project designed to provide a comprehensive view of sales performance, product performance, customer demographics, and regional revenue.

The dashboard transforms raw business data into meaningful visual insights that can support **data-driven decision-making, performance monitoring, customer understanding, and revenue optimization**.

The dashboard is organized into four major analytical sections:

- 📈 Sales Overview
- 🛍️ Product Analysis
- 👥 Customer Insight
- 🌍 Store & Regional Insight

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Monitor overall sales and revenue performance.
- Track profit and quantity sold.
- Analyze year-over-year business growth.
- Identify high-performing products and categories.
- Understand customer demographics and purchasing patterns.
- Analyze revenue performance across countries and regions.
- Identify top and bottom-performing states.
- Provide management with actionable business insights through interactive visualizations.

---

# 📈 Dashboard Sections

## 1. Sales Overview

![Sales Overview](image%202%20%281%29.png)

The **Sales Overview** page provides a high-level summary of the company's financial and sales performance.

### Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | $55.8M |
| Total Profit | $32.7M |
| Total Quantity Sold | 198K |
| Total Customers | 12K |

### Key Insights

- Total revenue reached **$55.8M**, representing a **12.1% increase** compared with the previous year.
- Total profit stood at **$32.7M**, also showing a **12.1% year-over-year increase**.
- Approximately **198K units** were sold, representing a **12.6% increase**.
- The customer base reached approximately **12K customers**, reflecting **5.0% growth**.
- North America generated the highest profit among the displayed continents.
- Monthly profit margin analysis shows fluctuations throughout the year, with stronger performance during selected months.
- The dashboard highlights the **Top 10 revenue-generating products**, making it easier to identify key contributors to total revenue.

## 2. Product Analysis

The **Product Analysis** page focuses on product-level performance, category profitability, quantity sold, and brand performance.

### Analysis Areas

- Orders by Product Name
- Profit by Category
- Quantity Sold by Category
- Revenue and Profit by Product
- Orders by Brand

### Key Insights

The analysis allows stakeholders to:

- Identify products generating the highest number of orders.
- Compare profitability across different product categories.
- Determine which categories have the highest sales volume.
- Examine the relationship between revenue and profit.
- Identify brands generating the highest number of orders.
- Discover products and categories that may require additional marketing or strategic attention.

### Product Performance

The dashboard provides a comparison between product revenue and profit, helping management understand which products contribute most effectively to overall business performance.

# 👥 3. Customer Insight

![Customer Insight](image%202%20(2).png)

The **Customer Insight** dashboard examines the company's customer base from geographical, age, and gender perspectives.

### Customer Analysis

The dashboard includes:

- Customers by Continent
- Orders by Age Category
- Customers by Gender
- Total Revenue by Country

### Customer Demographics

The customer analysis provides insights into:

- Geographic distribution of customers.
- Customer purchasing patterns across age groups.
- Gender distribution within the customer base.
- Countries contributing significantly to overall revenue.

### Key Observation

North America represents the largest customer base among the continents displayed, followed by Europe and Australia.

The age analysis also provides a breakdown of customers into:

- Old
- Middle Age
- Young

This segmentation can support targeted marketing campaigns and customer retention strategies.
